Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

At Tweet2Skill, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our browser extension and website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary data to provide our services:

  • Email Address and Identity Information: Collected when you sign in via Google OAuth. This is used solely to verify your subscription/Pro Credits status.
  • Scraped Content: When you explicitly trigger a generation, the extension scrapes the page structure and sends it to our serverless endpoint to be formatted into markdown. We do not store or keep this scraped content after generation is completed.
  • API Credentials (Optional): If you use Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) mode, your Gemini API key is stored securely in your browser's local storage and is never sent to our servers.

2. Data Usage

We certify that:

  • We do not sell user data to third parties.
  • We do not use user data for any purposes unrelated to the core functionality of generating developer instructions.
  • We do not store your browsing history or track your activity.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party integrations:

  • Google API Services: For OAuth authentication and AI generation.
  • Stripe: For processing one-time Pro purchases securely. No billing details ever touch our servers.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us at support@hero-apps.com.
